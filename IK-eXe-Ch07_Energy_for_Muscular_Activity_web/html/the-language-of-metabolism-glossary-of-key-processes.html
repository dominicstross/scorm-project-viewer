The Language of Metabolism – Glossary of Key Processes

The Language of Metabolism – Glossary of Key Processes


To effectively manage fuel sources in clinical or performance settings, one must master the terminology of metabolism. Metabolism is divided into two strategic categories: the breakdown of molecules for energy and the storage or creation of molecules.

Energy Release (Catabolism) 

  • Catabolism refers to the breakdown of stores to release energy.
  • Glycogenolysis: The breakdown of stored glycogen into glucose.
  • Lipolysis: The breakdown of stored fats (triglycerides) into free fatty acids.
  • Protein Breakdown: The conversion of protein into amino acids for energy (this is less common).


Energy Storage & Creation (Anabolism) 

  • Anabolism refers to the building of stores or the creation of new fuel sources.
  • Glycogenesis: The synthesis of glycogen from glucose (storage).
  • Lipogenesis: The creation of fat from excess carbohydrates or protein.
  • Protein Synthesis: The building of body proteins from amino acids.
  • Gluconeogenesis: The creation of new glucose from non-carbohydrate sources (such as amino acids).

A simple way to master this language is to look at the suffix: "-lysis" means to break, while "-genesis" means to create. Understanding these processes allows a practitioner to identify whether the body is in a state of energy release or storage.

Study Questions

Distinguish between Catabolism and Anabolism.

Catabolism is the process of breaking down molecules to release energy, while Anabolism is the process of building or storing molecules.


What is the metabolic process of breaking down stored muscle glycogen? 

 Glycogenolysis.


Define Gluconeogenesis and identify a potential source for it. 

The creation of new glucose from non-carbohydrate sources, such as amino acids.

 

What is the difference between Glycogenesis and Glycogenolysis? 

Glycogenesis is the creation of glycogen for storage, while glycogenolysis is the breakdown of stored glycogen for use.


What does the suffix "-lysis" signify?

To break.


What is the breakdown of stored fats called? 

Lipolysis.


What is the process of building body proteins from amino acids? 

Protein Synthesis.


 

Under what conditions might Lipogenesis occur? 

When there is an excess of carbohydrates or protein in the diet.


 

What does the suffix "-genesis" signify? 

To create.


 

Is Protein Breakdown a common metabolic pathway for energy? 

No; it is considered less common compared to carb and fat use.
